System Process

How It Works

Seven sequential stages take an examination paper from faculty upload to secure, authorized access on exam day.

📤
Step 01

Faculty Creates Paper

The process begins when a faculty member logs into their role-specific dashboard and uploads the examination question paper. The system accepts standard document formats and immediately begins the secure pipeline.

🎓 Role: Faculty
🤖
Step 02

AI Processing

Once uploaded, the AI engine automatically processes each question. Using Natural Language Processing, the system paraphrases questions to enhance security — the meaning and difficulty are preserved while the phrasing is modified, making early leaks far less useful.

🤖 AI NLP Engine
✅
Step 03

Exam Cell Review

Authorized exam cell personnel access the AI-processed paper in a read-only secure viewer. They review the content for accuracy, completeness, and quality, then either approve it to proceed or request changes from the faculty.

🏛 Role: Exam Cell
🔒
Step 04

Encryption

Upon approval, the Security Officer triggers AES-256-CBC encryption on the paper. The content is converted into ciphertext that is unreadable without the decryption key — even if intercepted, the data is meaningless to any unauthorized party.

🛡 AES-256-CBC Encryption
🔑
Step 05

OTP Generation

A cryptographically secure One-Time Password is generated and delivered only to pre-authorized users. The OTP has a defined expiration window — it cannot be reused, shared, or transferred. This creates a time-locked access gate.

⏱ Time-bound · Single-use
👁
Step 06

Secure Access

Only authorized users who possess a valid, unexpired OTP can access the paper through the Secure Viewer. The viewer displays content in a controlled environment — no printing, no copying, no screenshots. Every access is logged in real time.

🖥 Secure Viewer · Activity Log
📥
Step 07

Distribution

On exam day, the paper is securely downloaded and shared with the university's authorized personnel. The download event is logged, timestamped, and associated with the user's identity — creating a complete chain of custody from creation to distribution.

🏫 Role: Admin · Final Stage
