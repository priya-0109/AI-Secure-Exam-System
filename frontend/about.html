About The Project

The AI-Powered Secure Examination Paper Protection System is designed to eliminate examination paper leaks through advanced, layered security mechanisms.

The system combines Artificial Intelligence, Cyber Security, Encryption, and Authentication technologies to ensure confidentiality and integrity throughout the entire examination process — from paper creation by faculty to secure distribution on exam day.
