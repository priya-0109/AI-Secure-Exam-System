The Problem

Why This Project?

Examination paper leaks can compromise the integrity of educational institutions, damage student careers, and erode public trust in academic systems.

Our system provides a secure, end-to-end workflow to protect papers from the moment of creation through final distribution — with full audit trails and role-based accountability.

🔐

Zero Leak Tolerance

Multi-layer encryption ensures papers are never exposed in plain text.

👁

Full Audit Trail

Every access, approval, and action is logged and traceable.

⏱

Time-Bound Access

OTP keys expire automatically, preventing reuse or theft.

🤖

AI Enhanced

AI paraphrasing adds an extra layer of security to question integrity.

Technology Stack

Key Technologies

🤖

Artificial Intelligence

NLP-powered paraphrasing engine that rewrites questions while preserving their academic intent and difficulty level.

🔒

AES-256 Encryption

Military-grade Advanced Encryption Standard protects paper content from unauthorized access at rest and in transit.

🔑

OTP Verification

One-Time Passwords grant time-limited access to verified, authorized users only — keys auto-expire after use.

🛡

Cyber Security

Intrusion detection, suspicious activity monitoring, and anomaly alerts protect against unauthorized access attempts.

📊

Monitoring Dashboard

Real-time activity feed gives security officers complete visibility into every system event and user action.

💾

SQLite Database

Lightweight, reliable, embedded database stores all paper records, logs, OTPs, and user data securely.
